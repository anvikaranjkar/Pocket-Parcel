# Image credits

Pocket Parcel uses object photographs and transparent cutouts as decorative item artwork. The assets are stored locally in public/cutouts so shared parcels never depend on third-party hotlinks.

- Tea cup: Ian Pegg, “Cup of tea.png”, CC0, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cup_of_tea.png
- Digital camera: Bernie / Steffen Schmitz, “Nikon D7000 Digital SLR Camera 03 rework.png”, public domain, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nikon_D7000_Digital_SLR_Camera_03_rework.png
- Chocolate-chip cookie: Evan-Amos / Zuissii, “Choc-Chip-Cookie.png”, public domain, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Choc-Chip-Cookie.png
- Rose: PurePNG, “White Roses”, CC0: https://purepng.com/photo/18861/white-roses
- Tulip: PurePNG, “Tulip”, CC0: https://purepng.com/photo/18901/tulip
- Sunflower source: Uwe W., “Sunflower as PNG.png”, public domain, Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunflower_as_PNG.png
- Cassette, open book, ticket, daffodil and lavender sources: Rawpixel free PNG collection: https://www.rawpixel.com/
- Lily and pillow sources: TOPpng personal-use PNG collection: https://toppng.com/
- Typewriter source: PNGWing: https://www.pngwing.com/
- Vintage postcard paper: original Pocket Parcel raster asset created with the built-in image-generation workflow.

Several downloaded files advertised transparency but contained a baked checkerboard or photographic background. Those source photographs were background-extracted for this project; the underlying objects were retained and the edited files are included as PNGs.
